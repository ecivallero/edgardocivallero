Editorial and publication pathways

My work circulates through several editorial spaces, each with a distinct purpose. Some are independent imprints or platforms that I direct; others are external journals, publishers, and institutions.

Wayrachaki Editora

An independent, open-access imprint for books on critical librarianship, archives, memory, classification, and situated forms of knowledge.

Explore Wayrachaki Editora

El Zorro de Abajo Editora

An independent, open-access imprint devoted to music, oral memory, sound, traditional instruments, and organological research.

Explore El Zorro de Abajo Editora

Estudio paramuno

Fiction, photography, illustration, and other visual or material publications developed outside the two editorial imprints.

Explore Estudio paramuno

Instrumentarium

The principal platform for my books, articles, research materials, and public work on music and musical instruments.

Visit Instrumentarium
